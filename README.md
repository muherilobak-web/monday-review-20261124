A one-line review of my own Monday, written for the "Monday, Reviewed in One Line" bounty: "One star for a Monday that held a 9 o'clock meeting about a meeting and billed the rest of the day as productivity; would not recommend." The deadpan product-review format was chosen because it forces the line to be a review of the day itself, and its one concrete defect — a 9 o'clock meeting about another meeting — is the kind of Monday detail every office worker recognises instantly, which is what makes the verdict repeatable the next morning.
